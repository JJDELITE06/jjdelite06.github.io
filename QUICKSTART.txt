ClipVault 0.4.0 - Windows replay recorder

INSTALL
Intel/AMD Windows 10/11: ClipVault-0.4.0-Setup-x64.exe
Windows 11 on ARM: ClipVault-0.4.0-Setup-arm64.exe
Windows 10 on ARM is not supported by the x64 capture engine.
Run setup, then open ClipVault from the desktop or Start menu.
The installer is currently unsigned; Windows may show a publisher warning.
No account, Node.js, or development tools are needed.

FIRST LAUNCH
Keep internet connected while ClipVault automatically sets up its recording
engine (about 88 MiB). Setup progress appears in the window. If setup fails,
check your connection and click Start buffer to retry. After setup, local
recording works offline. Portable ZIP users must extract the whole ZIP first.

RECORD
1. In Recorder settings, choose Screen to record.
2. Choose audio sources and save shortcuts. Click Apply settings while stopped.
3. Click Start buffer before playing.
4. Press Ctrl+Shift+F8 (default), your custom shortcut, or your selected Xbox
   button when a moment happens. The Save button and tray menu work too.
5. Open clips folder to find the MP4, then share that file however you prefer.

The default is 30 seconds, 720p, 30 FPS. The Gaming preset reduces workload.
QUALITY: Choose 1440p and 120 FPS separately, or use the Quality preset
(1440p / 120 FPS / 80 Mbps / Auto encoder). Apply settings while stopped.
Resolution caps: 720p, 1080p, 1440p. FPS: 30, 60, 120. Bitrate: 25-120 Mbps.
Smaller screens retain their aspect ratio and are not upscaled. 120 FPS
requires sufficient source frames and PC/encoder headroom. Watch the actual
capture FPS; reduce settings if the encoder falls behind. Audio now uses
a continuous sample clock and larger ordered batches to reduce choppiness.
Recording still uses PC resources; gameplay impact varies by hardware/game.
Xbox Share support depends on controller, connection, firmware and Windows;
physical Share-button and Fortnite performance verification remain pending.

Closing the window keeps recording in the tray. Quit ClipVault exits fully.
Unplugging/changing the selected monitor pauses recording; choose a screen
and start again. System audio includes all PC sound, not just the chosen game.
Clips default to Videos/ClipVault. Uninstall preserves clips and preferences.
GOOGLE DRIVE (OPTIONAL)
Local saving is the default and needs no account. In Your clips. Your storage.,
click Connect Google Drive and finish sign-in in your system browser.
Choose Keep local + upload manually or Keep local + upload new clips
automatically, then Apply upload options. Automatic mode only queues future
saves; choose older MP4s explicitly. Every upload keeps the local file.
Uploads go directly to your private ClipVault folder in your own Google Drive.
The separate upload limit defaults to 5 Mbps (adjustable 1-50 Mbps).
Uploads pause while the recording buffer is active by default, to avoid
competing for your internet connection while gaming. Stop the buffer to upload.
Pause/resume, progress, retries and queue removal are available in the app.
Disconnect clears the local connection/queue and attempts Google revocation;
this may also disconnect ClipVault on your other PCs. It never deletes clips.
Google storage limits apply. Local-only recording still works offline.
Built-in trimming, ClipVault-web login/feed upload and game detection are not
included in this release.
