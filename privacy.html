ClipVault privacy and Google Drive

Updated October 18, 2026 · desktop recorder and download website

ClipVault saves gameplay clips on your own PC. Version 0.4.0 includes an optional Google Drive connection. Local-only saving remains the default; each person signs into their own Google account if they choose Drive uploads.

Local recording

The recorder captures the display and audio sources you enable and writes temporary buffer segments and completed MP4 clips to local storage. Your screen selection, shortcuts, audio settings and output folder are stored on your PC. Closing or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete your saved clips.

Optional Google Drive connection

Connecting uses Google's sign-in page in your system browser. ClipVault does not receive or store your Google password. The integration requests the per-file drive.file permission to create and manage the Drive files used by ClipVault. It does not request access to your entire Drive, Gmail, contacts or calendar.

A connection may create a ClipVault folder in your Drive. No clip is uploaded until you choose an upload mode and either select a clip manually or enable automatic uploads for future saves. Each user connects their own Google account.

What is used and where it goes

  • Selected MP4 contents, filenames and sizes are sent directly from your PC to Google Drive to create the copies you request.
  • ClipVault reads metadata for its folder and upload files to confirm completion and resume interrupted transfers. It stores local queue paths, file identity, progress and Google file/folder identifiers for that purpose.
  • Google access/refresh tokens and resumable-session details are kept in an encrypted local vault using Windows account protection (DPAPI through Electron safeStorage). They are not sent to the website, placed in public downloads or exposed to the app's interface.
  • ClipVault does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, train AI models on it or send it to a ClipVault upload server. Google processes files under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your controls and deletion

Local-only saving is the default. Manual and automatic Drive uploads keep a local copy. Uploads pause while the recording buffer is active by default; you can change the average bandwidth limit and pause/resume transfers.

Removing a queue entry stops tracking that upload and never deletes a clip. Disconnecting clears the connection and upload queue on this PC and attempts to revoke Google authorization. Google's revocation can also affect other ClipVault connections for that account. If the connection is offline, you can revoke access directly in Google Account connections.

Uploaded files remain in your Drive until you delete them there. Local files remain on your PC until you delete them. Deleting one copy does not automatically delete the other. Google storage limits apply.

Download website and support

The download website uses no ClipVault analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub hosts the site and release downloads and may process request information under its own privacy policy. Diagnostic recorder logs remain local and should not contain media or authorization tokens.

For privacy questions or deletion guidance, use the ClipVault support page. Do not post recordings, passwords or authorization tokens in a public issue.
